NSBB is a digitally run tax advisory firm with an office in Berlin-Zehlendorf. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tax advisor in Berlin cost?

Fees are based on the German Tax Advisor Remuneration Regulation (StBVV). After a short initial conversation, we provide a transparent quote tailored to your needs.

Can I switch from my current tax advisor to NSBB easily?

Yes. You can switch at any time; we coordinate the handover of your documents and data for you.

Do I have to come in person, or can everything be done digitally?

Collaboration can be fully digital. In-person meetings at our Zehlendorf office are of course also possible.

Do you handle international matters from Berlin?

Yes. We manage cross-border questions independently and, where required, together with our worldwide TGS network.

Who do you work with in Berlin?

Companies and corporate groups, freelancers and private individuals with demanding tax questions – both national and international.
