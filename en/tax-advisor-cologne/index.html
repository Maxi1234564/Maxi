Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tax advisor in Cologne cost?

Our fees are based on the German Tax Advisor Remuneration Regulation (StBVV). In an initial meeting we clarify your needs and provide a transparent quote.

Is switching tax advisors complicated?

No. We handle the coordination with your previous advisor and the transfer of your data, so the switch is smooth for you.

Can the advice be handled entirely digitally?

Yes. You can work with us fully digitally – in-person meetings at the Rheinauhafen are always possible.

Do you also advise on international tax matters?

Yes. We handle international matters independently and, where needed, together with our TGS partners worldwide.

Which clients do you support in Cologne?

Companies of all legal forms and sizes, freelancers and private individuals with demanding tax questions.
